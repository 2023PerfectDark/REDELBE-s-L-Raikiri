The list describes resource mappings; some slots may not be separate selectable
entries in your game mode. Random keeps its normal preview picture.

Changing stages or variants stops the previous video/audio, returns to the new
picture and name, then fades in that slot's video after the name disappears.
A missing video leaves the normal picture. You can add videos while the game is
running. Revisit the slot after replacing an already-playing file.
