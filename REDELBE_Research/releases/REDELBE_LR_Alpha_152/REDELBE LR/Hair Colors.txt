HAIR COLOR STORAGE AND CPU COLORS

In REDELBE LR/REDELBE.ini:
[HairColors]
storage_mode = cache

Close the game and run Tools/Prepare Hair Colors.exe after changing storage_mode.
Cache mode generates colors when needed and retains at most 1 GiB (1,073,741,824
bytes) of generated texture containers. Least recently used files are removed;
saved wardrobe choices remain. First use of an uncached color can pause briefly.
The setup offers to remove the old generated full palette to reclaim space.
Helper files, old backups and preserved full-palette files are outside this limit.
Launch through REDELBE afterward to synchronize. Re-run preparation after game
updates if the cache reports that its source resources have changed.

For the complete approximately 27 GB palette, use storage_mode = full and run
Prepare Hair Colors again. Full preparation is required for random CPU colors:
[Random]
enable_random_hair_colors = true
[RandomHairColor]
probability = 35

Range: 1..100 percent. 1 is a 1% chance; 100 always requests a different custom
color. This changes colors, not hairstyles, and leaves saved wardrobe choices
unchanged. Currently applies to offline Versus CPU fighters, including AI vs AI.
Shared hair resources with conflicting fighter colors can fall back to default.
Random CPU colors are ignored in cache mode. Restart after INI changes.
